# DASPi 10-camera trigger distributor — Rev L Compact Dual

Rev L corrects the copper forge orientation. The carrier, holes, copper grooves and electrical routing are identical to Rev K.

## Rev L forge correction

Rev K copied the copper coordinates directly into the upright forge. Turning that tool face-down introduced an unwanted mirror. Rev L builds one left-to-right reflection into the printed forge:

`x_forge = 110 mm - x_copper`

`y_forge = y_copper`

The reflection is applied to:

- Every raised trace rib.
- Every raised pad.
- Every alignment pin.

When the finished forge is rotated 180° about its Y axis, the physical flip supplies the second reflection and returns every contact feature to the copper coordinates.

## Printing and use

1. Run `DASPi_10Cam_Trigger_Distributor_RevL_Compact_Dual.FCMacro` in FreeCAD.
2. Export and print `Press_Tool` in the orientation generated by the macro.
3. **Do not mirror the tool in the slicer.** Rev L already contains the required mirror.
4. After printing, turn the forge face-down with a left-to-right, 180° rotation about its Y axis.
5. Do not flip it top-to-bottom about its X axis.
6. Align the four pins with the carrier mounting holes and verify the complete perimeter and several asymmetric pads before pressing the copper.

The FreeCAD object is labelled `PRINT - Rev L Copper Forge - X MIRRORED` and contains orientation instructions in its Forge properties.

## Carrier retained from Rev K

- Carrier size: 110 × 48 × 2.4 mm.
- U1/U2 are back-mounted SN74LVC245A devices.
- Both IC notches face the bottom board edge.
- Viewed from the copper side, pin 1 is lower-left and pin 20 is lower-right.
- DIR pin 1 is connected to 1.8 V.
- A1–A8 pins 2–9 share the trigger input.
- GND pin 10 and `/OE` pin 19 use insulated ground jumpers.
- VCC pin 20 is connected to 1.8 V.
- B5–B1 pins 14–18 drive the five connectors through 68 Ω resistors.
- C3/C6 are beside the corrected physical pin-20 locations.

## Connector construction retained

- Ten JST B02B-PASK-1 connectors.
- Two 0.80 mm lead holes per connector on 2.00 mm centres.
- No full-depth rectangular connector openings.
- Shallow 0.80 mm underside seating pockets leave 1.60 mm local carrier thickness.
